## What was done since report 10

1. **`iterate` for monoidal operations** (36 theorems).  A commutative-monoid theory for the
   native enumeration product: `cmonoid_on`, closure of `group_word_product`, extraction of
   the j-th factor (`gwp_pullout`), bijections (`bij_remove`, `bij_skip`, `bij_restrict`) and
   invariance of the product under any enumeration (`gwp_bij_invariant`); then
   `iterate_op` over the empty set, membership, support idempotence and the insert recursion
   `iterate_op (s :\/: {x}) f = op (f x) (iterate_op s f)` (`iterate_op_adjoin`), and
   `cmonoid_of_monoidal` (the native `monoidal` conjunction plus closure gives a commutative
   monoid whose `group_identity` is `neutral_of`).  On the literal side, `hl_ITSET_char`: the
   recursion function chosen by HOL Light's `ITSET` agrees on finite sets with any function
   satisfying the same recursion.  `hl_iterate_compat` is conditional on `monoidal op`
   (registry side condition; HOL Light's `ITSET` is a choice and its value is arbitrary for
   non-monoidal operations), and side conditions are now matched up to alpha-equivalence and
   rebuilt from hypotheses that were registered as split conjuncts.
2. **Compatibility theorems** (321 total; 298 at report 10).
