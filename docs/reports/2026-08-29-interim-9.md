## What was done since report 8

1. **Side conditions derived structurally.**  `finite s` is now derived from the hypotheses in
   scope by structure: finite hypotheses, singletons, adjoined elements, unions, replacements
   (images), separations, differences, intersections, subsets of finite sets (`s c= t`
   hypotheses) and bounded segments of `omega`; nonemptiness of `{a}` and `SetAdjoin s a` is
   derived structurally as well.  This certifies most `CARD`, `sup`/`inf` and finite-image
   theorems whose finiteness follows from the hypotheses.
2. **Functions into subsets** (`f : K -> A -> bool`).  The literal `f :e 2 :^: A :^: K` and the
   native `g :e Power A :^: K` are bound together with the pointwise hypothesis
   `hl_rep A (f x) = g x` (`imp_forall_repfun`, `imp_exists_repfun` and their converses; the
   native side is instantiated with `fun x :e K => hl_chi A (g x)`, the literal side with
   `fun x :e K => hl_rep A (f x)`); applications `f x` are related as representations
   (`KRepFun`), lambdas into subsets in meta positions likewise, and compatibility statements of
   subset-valued function arguments now carry the representation hypothesis
   (`forall x :e K, hl_rep A (l x) = f x`), e.g. `cartesian_product`, `disjoint_union`.
3. **Comprehensions with two pattern variables** (`{(x,y) | P x y}`, `{s :*: t | …}`): the
   literal `GSPEC` with a nested existential is related to the elaborator's family union
   `\/_ x :e A, {F x y | y :e B, P x y}` (`hl_gspec_generic2`, `gspec_famunion_form`).
4. **Explicit pair projections** `(a,b) 0`, `(a,b) 1` are reduced in every native subterm the
   bridge sees (the elaborator's statement-level rewrite pass does the same); this certified the
   `dist` group and paired arguments generally.
5. **Compatibility theorems** (270 total): `I`, `int_ge`, `num_mod` (integer congruences of
   naturals, by `int_SNo_cases`), `FILTER`, `set_of_list`, `list_of_set` (both choices have the
   same specification; finiteness is forced on both sides), `ALLPAIRS`, `PAIRWISE`, `NONE`,
   `cartesian_product`, `disjoint_union`, with the native sequence lemmas `seq_filter_nil/cons`,
   `seq_set_nil/cons`, `seq_forall_cons`, `seq_pairwise_nil/cons`.
6. **Generator.**  Partially applied binary meta-variables (`seq_ex (P x) l`), conditionals at
   subset types (`hl_rep_if`), the compat index of type-specialised registry entries (carrier
   arguments now come from the entry's own scheme), a guarded build/regenerate/check/commit
   cycle (commit `b1e309e` had briefly committed a non-compiling `bridge.ml`; fixed in the next
   commit).

## Remaining blockers (theorems, 518 public uncertified)

`iterate` for general monoidal operations (42): the native `iterate_op` is a product over an
enumeration of the support; relating it to HOL's insert-recursive `ITSET` needs a
commutative-monoid reordering theory for `group_word_product` and a `monoidal` side condition
(the compat statement is only true for monoidal operations).  `PCROSS`/cart/`finite_image`/
`tybit0` types (about 45; outside the literal interface), images over sets of subsets
(`{s :*: t | t :e Power A, t :e f}`, 17), `CARD` and `sup`/`inf` without derivable side conditions
(43; mostly conditional inside the statement, not certifiable with conditional compat theorems),
`EL`/`HD`/`TL` (15; need conditional compat theorems with an index-range side condition), `ALL2`,
`REVERSE`, `list_of_seq`, `LAST`, `BUTLAST`, `ZIP` (36; index arithmetic on `finseq`), numeral
arithmetic rewrites (9), `gcd`/`lcm`/`coprime` (20; uniqueness of the gcd specification), binders
over triples (6), `nadd`/`hreal` construction types (about 30).
