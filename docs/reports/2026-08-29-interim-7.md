## What was done since report 6

1. **Representations.**  Sets of subsets (`(A->bool)->bool`) get the nested representation
   `hl_rep2 A F = {hl_rep A u | u :e hl_rep (2 :^: A) F}` (relation kind `KRep2`, binder lemmas
   `imp_forall_sub2`…, `hl_chi2`, injectivity), with nested instances of the set-theoretic
   compatibility theorems (`hl_IN_compat_pow`, `INSERT`, `EMPTY`, `UNIV`, `UNION`, `INTER`, `DIFF`,
   `DELETE`, `SUBSET`, `PSUBSET`, `FINITE`, `INFINITE`, `DISJOINT`) proved from the depth-1 ones and
   images of injections; `UNIONS`/`INTERS`/`UNION_OF`/`INTERSECTION_OF` compat.  Meta-predicates
   over subsets (and over sets of subsets) are related through the representation
   (`imp_forall_pred_rep` with an explicit carrier/representation pair; compatibility hypotheses
   `l x = 1 <-> P (hl_rep A x)`).
2. **Compatibility theorems** (98 → 222): `sup`/`inf` and `has_sup`/`has_inf` (conditional on
   the existence of a least upper / greatest lower bound; side conditions are now *derived*
   from hypotheses: nonempty + bounded via `lub_exists_R`, guarded bounds, membership, finite
   sets via `finite_max_exists`), integer `div`/`rem` (existence from `quotient_remainder_int`,
   uniqueness by integer discreteness and monotonicity; the native `rem_int x 0 = x` now matches
   `INT_DIVISION_0`), lists (`CONS`, `LENGTH`, `APPEND`, `MAP`, `MEM`, `ALL` by `seq_induct`
   from generated specification lemmas, which now exist for polymorphic recursive constants),
   `one`, `SOME`, `int_gt`, `ARBITRARY`, `dimindex`, `minimal`, unique existence.
3. **Generator.**  `?!` through the elaborator's expansion; side conditions instantiated with
   native placeholders; unique hypothesis names; canonical template constants; scheme-based
   instantiation; Boolean-term lambda bodies; equality of predicates as pointwise `<->`;
   Hilbert choice in term position (`choose_in_ext`); partial applications of mapped constants
   in meta positions by eta-expansion (`pw_eta_pred`/`pw_eta_fun`); a catch-all so that a
   generator exception can never abort a run.
4. **Selftest**: golden fixtures are compared after certification finalisation, so they assert
   the certification results (`make test` had been silently failing on this since report 5).

## Defects found by certification (cumulative, see DESIGN §21.7)

Two regressions were committed with a failing `sets` shard during this period (a depth-2
predicate binder, and a `grep|tail` pipeline hiding `check_cert.sh`'s exit status); both were
caught by the next check and repaired within the same session.  Megalodon lessons recorded:
`+` is right-associative in the God1 notation table; a statement/proof mismatch in a goal that
contains `*` makes Megalodon unfold `mul_SNo` and hang instead of failing (bisect with
`Admitted.` truncations); `apply` with a `*`-containing motive hangs; applied proof-level
lambdas need an expected type (use a lemma that takes the pointwise proof as an argument).
