## What was done since report 14

1. **Model-soundness theorems (§21.4), `530690d`, `a40d9ba`.**  The literal statements of the
   characterizing theorems of the primitive interface are proved in
   `mglib/literal/model_theorems.mg` (41 theorems, all `Qed`): the 21 `realax.ml` axioms
   (`REAL_ADD_SYM` … `REAL_COMPLETE` via God1's `lub_of_bounds`), `PAIR_EQ`, `PAIR_SURJECTIVE`,
   `ETA_AX` (`Pi_eta`), `SELECT_AX` (`choose_in_spec`), `BOOL_CASES_AX`, `INFINITY_AX`
   (`ordsucc`), `NOT_SUC`, `SUC_INJ`, `num_INDUCTION`, `num_Axiom` (`nat_primrec`, uniqueness by
   induction and `Pi_ext`), `one_axiom`, `NOT_CONS_NIL`, `CONS_11`, `list_INDUCT`,
   `list_RECURSION` (a paramorphism through pairs `(tail, value)` folded with `seq_foldr`),
   `option_INDUCT/RECURSION`, `sum_INDUCT/RECURSION` (`Unj`).  The generator uses a model theorem
   only when its statement is textually the generated literal statement; then the certification
   module ends `hlt_N` and `N` with `Qed`.  38 public theorems are thereby free of any admission
   (`SELECT_AX`, `list_RECURSION` and `INFINITY_AX` are proved but not bridged: `@` applied to a
   variable predicate, a ternary function binder, no public statement).
2. **Defect found by a model theorem**: `hl_INR` in `model.mg` bound its carrier parameters in the
   order (A, B) while the literal layer orders them by first occurrence in the generic type
   (`INR : B -> (A,B)sum`, so `hl_INR B A`); every INR theorem had been untypeable.  Fixed
   (`a40d9ba`); `INL`/`INR` compatibility theorems added (`d0a0de4`), which certified
   `sum_INDUCT`/`sum_RECURSION`.
3. **Coverage** (2369 → 2417): products/sums over `finprod`/`finsum` (`nproduct`, `product`,
   `iproduct`, `isum`; `finprod` is `iterate_op` over `R`, `iterate_op` depends on its carrier only
   through the neutral element and the group identity), the standard commutative monoids derived
   as `iterate` side conditions, replay of the elaborator rewrites `idx_n_def`/`idx_def`
   (convertible), `vacuous_forall` (instantiation at a canonical inhabitant) and `dimindex_one`
   (Leibniz), the `?!` binder typing bug (`EXISTS_UNIQUE` group), finiteness of products, power
   sets, enumerations and `seq_set`, enumeration order `{s,t}`/`{a,b,c}`/`{a,b,c,d}`, `seq_rev`
   non-emptiness, comprehensions with a subset-valued body (`hl_rep2` on the result), and the
   compatibility theorems `SURJ`, `CURRY`, `DECIMAL`.
