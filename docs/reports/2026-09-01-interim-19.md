## What was built

- **`mglib/native/logic.mg`** — the native `<->`-congruence toolkit (connectives, bounded
  and subset quantifiers, prop quantifier, `iff_true_intro`/`iff_false_intro`, equality
  helpers), composed into the native contexts only (no clash with the literal bridge).
- **`Nativeproof.iff_congruence`** — structural congruence proof terms between similar
  props, descending shared connectives/binders, closing leaves from premise instances or
  equality-instance Leibniz motives.
- **`Nativeproof.prove_via_rewrites`** — rewrite-normalization of a goal to `True` with
  the recorded proof leaves as rules: nested clause splitting, plain facts as `p <-> True`,
  candidate lists with chain-level retry, general-prover termination.  Emitted as a claim
  chain over the intermediate forms — the God1-readable shape of a REWRITE_TAC proof.
- **Soundness fix**: `match_tm` rejects bindings that capture a binder variable (silent
  captures had produced wrong rewrite candidates; no checked proof depended on one).
