## What was done since report 13

1. **`EL` (commit `46ecd0a`).**  HOL Light's `EL` is total but only specified in range; the
   compatibility theorem is conditional on `n :e seq_len l` (witness: `HD (TL^n l)`, i.e.
   `nat_primrec l (fun _ r => hl_TL B r) n`, related to `seq_nth` by `tlk_shift`).  The generator
   derives the range side condition from hypotheses `n < LENGTH l` (also through `MAP` and
   `list_of_seq` via `seq_len_map`/`seq_len_mk`), from the typing of the index and reflexively.
   6 theorems (`EL`, `EL_APPEND`, `EL_MAP`, `EL_TL`, `LAST_EL`, `EL_CONS`-type) certified; the
   remaining `EL` theorems have no range premise (they hold in HOL for the unspecified values).
2. **Comprehensions over subsets with subset-valued bodies (`a82fe5a`).**  `{s :*: t | t IN f}`
   with `f` a set of subsets: the pattern variable is represented (`hl_rep A t`) in the pointwise
   proofs and the result by `hl_rep2` (`gspec_replsep_form_sub2`, `gspec_repl_form_sub2`,
   `gspec_sep_form_sub2`, which use the surjectivity of `hl_rep` onto `Power A` through `hl_chi`).
   The generator elaborates the native lambdas twice: once with the plain variable (the `P'`, `F'`
   of the form lemmas) and once with the variable replaced by its representation (the pointwise
   proofs).  15 theorems (`CROSS_INTERS`, `DIFF_UNIONS`, `FINITE_POWERSET`, `INTERS_UNIONS`,
   `UNIONS_MAXIMAL_SETS`, ...).
3. **Definition compatibility batch (`7c6a554`)**: `MEASURE`, `REST`, `INJ`, `BIJ` (through a
   characterization of the literal `SURJ`), `ITLIST` (three-argument bounded lambdas `lam3_Pi`/
   `lam3_beta`, `seq_foldr_in`), `REPLICATE` (`seq_replicate_0`/`seq_replicate_S` by finite-sequence
   extensionality), `>=_c`, `<_c` (God1's `SchroederBernstein`, `equip_sym`, `equip_atleastp`),
   `=_c` (bijection from HOL's `?!` formulation, default value `choose_in` outside the domain),
   `COUNTABLE`.  17 theorems.

Partially specified HOL constants (`ASSOC` has no `[]` clause, `MAP2` no clause for unequal
lengths) cannot be related to total native functions unconditionally; as for `EL`/`ZIP`/`HD`
they need a side condition, and only theorems whose hypotheses supply it can be certified
(`LENGTH_MAP2`, `EL_MAP2`); the definitional clauses themselves stay uncertified.
