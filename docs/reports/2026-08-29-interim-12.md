## What was done since report 11

1. **Lists.**  `REVERSE`, `list_of_seq` and `ZIP` (conditional on equal lengths; the witness
   for HOL Light's `HD`/`TL`-based specification iterates `TL` with `nat_primrec`), with the
   native index lemmas for `seq_rev`, `seq_append`, `seq_mk`, `seq_zip` and the arithmetic
   `nat_minus_succ`, `ordsucc_nat_pred`.
2. **Booleans as data.**  A formula in a data position (`P T`, `P F`) is related as
   `if p then 1 else 0` (`bool_data_of_iff`); Boolean variables used as data on either side of
   a logical-head equation (`(/\) = \p q. …`, `AND_DEF`, `IMP_DEF`, `OR_DEF`) and quantifiers as
   data (`(?) = \P. …`, `hl_exists_eq1`, `hl_forall_eq1`).
3. **Binders over ternary meta-predicates** (`hl_chip3`, `imp_forall_pred3`, …).
4. **Compatibility theorems**: `ISO`, `CHOICE`, `prime`, the cardinal comparison `<=_c`
   (`atleastp`; the literal witness is extended by `ARB` outside the domain), `pairwise` on
   sets of subsets; finiteness of separations with a membership conjunct
   (`{i :e A | i :e s /\ …} c= s`).
