# Interim report 17 — native proof synthesis begins (2026-08-31)

Phase change (new goal): translate *proofs*, with the bar that the results read as good
native Megalodon proofs in the God1 / `examples/form100/100thms_12.mg` style — not as the
uniform-model implants of the §22 pilot (which remain the semantic safety net: 840 Core
theorems `fully_proved`, recorded and re-validated on every cycle).

## What exists after day one (DESIGN §23)

- **Design**: §23 — architecture (step mining at named-lemma granularity, native transport
  on the rigid fragment, declarative emission), the no-alien-implant quality bar, phases
  N1–N5.
- **N1 analytics** (`tools/native_proof_stats.py`): on the 1 725 recorded Core proofs the
  kernel-rule stream compresses only 2.2× naively; the named-lemma skeleton and rigidity
  analysis identify where native synthesis is worthwhile; 516 recorded proofs are fully
  rigid.
- **N2 prover** (`lib/nativeproof.ml`, ~300 lines): bounded declarative prover over the
  native statement AST; God1 idioms only (`let`/`assume`/`prove`/`apply`/`exact`/`witness`,
  bullets, `andI`/`iffI`/`orIL`/`orIR`, inline Leibniz equality, first-order instantiation
  of quantified hypotheses).  **301 of 2 685 public Core theorems now carry generated native
  proofs; the whole set Megalodon-checks in 3 seconds against God1 + native prelude +
  public definitions only, and the public shards emit them with `Qed` (`natively_proved`)** (`generated/nativeproof/core/`, `tools/check_nativeproof.sh`,
  regenerated by `tools/update.sh`).

Two sample outputs, exactly as generated:

    Theorem EQ_SYM : forall A:set, forall x y :e A, x = y -> y = x.

    let x. assume Hx.

    exact (H (fun hl__u hl__v => hl__u = (x)) (fun q H => H)).
    Qed.

## Day-one increments

Ex falso, classical double negation (`xm`), `<>` negations, Leibniz transport along equality
hypotheses, congruence by motive replacement (149 → 158); premise machinery over the recorded
proof leaves with a `Qed`-only fixpoint; the emission switch — the public shards now carry
the generated proofs with `Qed` and the manifest records `natively_proved`; recursive
negation application, `andI`/`orIL`/`orIR` proof terms, unbounded-existential witnesses, the
congruence-motive fix, and native re-proving of `native_reuse` propositions (158 → 183);
then conclusion projection (`andEL`/`andER`), back-chaining for open pattern variables, the
built-in God1 premise table (`EmptyE`, `choose_in_in`), and the `False`-goal fall-through
(183 → 193); then God1 arithmetic/If_i premises, symmetric equational application, and
`choose_in` witnesses for nonempty carriers (193 → 207); then ex_intro proof terms, the If-split case rule, bidirectional transport,
and the fixpoint/fuel tuning (207 → 213, native-proof phase ~4.7 min); then the N3a sets machinery — Sep/boolean-set
membership construction and destructuring, `c=` intro, `set_ext` fallback (213 → 246); then Subq-hypothesis application and negation lambdas
(246 → 262); then derived ex falso, recursive setminus premises, subset-evidenced set_ext
and iff application (262 → 284, phase back to ~3.6 min); then False through negated
conclusions, depth-free structural rules, and hypothesis augmentation in lambda terms
(284 → 292 — DELETE finally; NPDEBUG tracing added); then guarded classical splits and
existential fall-through to or-elimination (292 → 298 — COMPL_COMPL, EXISTS_OR family); then witness mining by body matching plus
Subq_omega_int/choose_in_spec premises (298 → 301 — EXISTS_THM, SELECT_AX, int_of_num).
