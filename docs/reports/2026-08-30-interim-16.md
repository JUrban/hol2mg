## What was built (commits `d9390ad` … `c122e28`)

1. Proof-recording kernel (`hol_export/fusion_rec.ml`, generated from the pinned `fusion.ml` by
   `tools/make_fusion_rec.py`), shadow HOL Light directory, DAG statistics, exporter with
   hash-consed terms and post-ordered proof records (§22.1–22.2).

   `\/`, `==>`, `!`, `?`, `?!`, `COND`, the three axioms and the type-definition characterizations
   in uniform form, coherence lemmas (§22.3).
3. Importer (`lib/proofimport.ml`): closed uniform statement per node, one claim per shared
   inference, shared typing claims per subterm, closing of variables lost between premises and
   conclusion, capture-free binder opening, variables of equal name and different type renamed
   apart, coherence proof `LP[t] <-> U[t] = 1` discharging `hlt_N`; leaves as `hltu_M` (proved or
   admitted); `tools/cert_finalize.py` computes the closure (§22.4).
4. Pilot tooling (`tools/proof_pilot.sh`), artifacts under `generated/proofcert/`.

## Findings

- Megalodon handles proofs of several hundred to a thousand inferences as claim chains
  (≈ 10 s per MB of proof text); the text is dominated by the printed closed statements of the
  nodes (typing derivations are shared).  Single-use node inlining is not an option (unchecked
  proof lambdas need annotated binders).
- Parser/conversion pitfalls recorded in §22.4: bare-variable lambda bodies in proof-term
  position, eta-reduction of lambda arguments before comparison, binder order of the closed
  statements, name capture between bound and free variables of the same HOL name.
- The closure is limited by leaves above the export cap (456 of the 726 blocking leaves) and by a
  few definitional theorems with enormous recorded proofs (`is_int`: 162 111 inferences, blocking
  the whole `int` chain — discharged by a model theorem instead).  A cap-2 000 run (1 683
  theorems) and a targeted export of the large leaves (`int_add_th`, `MONOIDAL_ADD`, …) are the
  next steps; see §22.5 for the updated numbers.

## The `cart` slice (commits `c87c45b` … `356825b`)

The 93 public theorems without a literal statement were all from cart.ml.  Their literal
statements needed type definitions with *phantom* parameters (`finite_image A` is a subtype of
`num` whose predicate mentions `dimindex(:A)`); the literal layer now takes the parameters
from the predicate term.  Certifying them needed a second layering stage (DESIGN §21.9):
parametrised native carriers (`hl_ty_T_native`), a forward Leibniz conversion of statements and
typing lemmas, `carriers2.mg` / `_literal_typing2.mg` / `compat2.mg` after `compat.mg`, index
side conditions for `$` and `finite_index` (characterised on `1..dimindex N` only) derived
from the hypotheses, the abs/rep parameter order of multi-parameter type definitions, the
datatypes `tybit0`/`tybit1` modelled by the native index sets, and 15 carrier and 22 compat
lemmas (`lambda` by select uniqueness over the index range, `pastecart`/`fstcart`/`sndcart`
blockwise, `PCROSS` as a family union).  Two latent bugs surfaced and were fixed: a GSPEC
intermediate set built with a fixed binder name (captured when the HOL witness is named `v`,
`FINITE_CART`) and the abs/rep parameter order of `mk_cart`.

Result: `transport_checked` 2 455 → **2 528** (cart.ml 83 / 103; every public theorem of Core
now has a literal statement), then **2 547** with five more small groups (function values,
`EXTENSIONAL`, over-applied subsets, `COND_ABS`, `IMAGE` with subset-valued functions; DESIGN §21.9);
